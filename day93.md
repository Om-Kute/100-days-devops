🚀 Day 93/100 – Logging & Centralized Log Management
📝 Collect • Store • Search • Visualize • Troubleshoot

Day 93 focuses on Logging and Centralized Log Management, an important part of modern DevOps, observability, security, and production troubleshooting.

Applications and infrastructure generate large amounts of logs every day. Instead of checking logs individually on every server or container, centralized logging collects them into a common platform where engineers can search, filter, analyze, visualize, and troubleshoot system behavior.
